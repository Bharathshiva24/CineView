See the screen from your seat.

CineView is a free Chrome extension for District and BookMyShow. Pick a seat, click CineView, and watch the movie's trailer on the screen exactly as you'd see it from that row.

Download for Chrome View source on GitHub

Version 2.4.0. Free and open source. Works in Chrome, Edge, Brave and Arc.

Three clicks between you and a better seat.

  1. 1

    Choose seats as usual

    Open a movie on District or BookMyShow, pick a showtime and select your seats. CineView reads the seat map and works out where each seat sits in the hall.

  2. 2

    Click CineView

    Click the toolbar icon or press ⌥⇧S. A 3D hall opens from your seat's eye height, rows in front of you included.

  3. 3

    Watch the trailer from there

    The movie's official trailer plays on the screen. Compare seats on the map, check the score, then go back and book.

Know before you book.

Every seat gets a score out of 100 and four plain numbers: how much of your view the screen fills, distance in screen widths, neck angle and how far off-centre you are. Industry guidance puts the sweet spot at about 36–40° of your view; CineView tells you where you land.

  • Green, yellow or orange for each number, so you can scan it in a second
  • Sound balance from your position in the hall

Compare every seat in seconds.

The seat map in CineView mirrors the one you booked from. Click any seat, or use the arrow keys, and the camera glides there. Zoom in to read seat numbers and drag to move around large halls.

  • Booked seats are dimmed, your picks are highlighted
  • Pinch, the + and − buttons, or keys + and − to zoom

IMAX is not just a bigger Scope screen.

CineView detects the format from the showtime you pick, such as "10:30 PM · IMAX 3D", and draws the screen at its real proportions. Try them here.

Install CineView in under a minute.

CineView isn't on the Chrome Web Store yet, so you add it as an unpacked extension. It stays installed after you restart Chrome.

Download CineView 2.4.0

430 KB zip for Chrome, Edge, Brave and Arc.

  1. Unzip the download

    Double-click cineview-v2.4.0.zip. You'll get a folder named cineview-v2.4.0. Keep it somewhere it won't be deleted.

  2. Open the extensions page

    Paste this into Chrome's address bar. Browsers don't let websites open it directly.

    chrome://extensions

  3. Turn on Developer mode

    Use the switch in the top-right corner of the extensions page.

  4. Load the folder

    Click Load unpacked and choose the cineview-v2.4.0 folder. Pin CineView from the puzzle-piece menu so it's one click away.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. CineView is free and its code is public on GitHub.

Does it collect my data?

No. It runs in your browser, reads only the District or BookMyShow page you're on, and talks only to YouTube to find and play the trailer. There are no accounts, analytics or servers.

Which trailer does it play?

The one District or BookMyShow lists for the movie. If there isn't one, CineView searches YouTube and only accepts videos titled with the movie's name and "trailer" or "teaser". Reactions, reviews and songs are filtered out, and if nothing matches it tells you instead of guessing.

How accurate is the view?

Seat maps don't include real measurements, so CineView estimates the hall from the seat layout. If you know the theatre, adjust screen width, row spacing and stadium height in its settings.

Which sites does it work on?

District and BookMyShow seat selection pages, in any Chromium browser.

Is CineView made by District or BookMyShow?

No. CineView is an independent project and isn't affiliated with either company.
